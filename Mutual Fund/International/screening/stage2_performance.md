# Stage 2 — Performance Filters (International Funds)

**Input:** 10 Stage-1 survivors (all active, all open to SIP) | **Result:** 10 → **8 shortlisted**

**The core adaptation:** skill is judged **vs each fund's own benchmark / peer group**, never on absolute cross-geography CAGR (which only measures which market ran hottest). See [methodology.md](methodology.md) §3.

## The Two Eliminations

| Fund | Why eliminated |
|------|----------------|
| **Franklin Asian Equity** | **Persistent peer-laggard** — below its sub-category over 1Y, 3Y *and* 5Y (0.83 / 0.82 / 0.75), the weakest 3Y rolling return (13.8%), and the lowest 5Y CAGR of the survivors (7.5%). Its high Sharpe (2.09) reflects low *absolute* volatility, not outperformance. The Asia-ex-Japan mandate is also covered, more cleanly, by the EM funds. No distinct role to justify keeping a serial laggard. |
| **Edelweiss ASEAN** | **Narrowest mandate + weakest peer-relative** — single-region ASEAN bet, the lowest 3Y vs-sub-category (0.62) and 3Y CAGR (14.6%) of the set, smallest AUM (₹146 Cr), and the priciest ER (1.60%). A narrow regional satellite with no skill edge; redundant given broad EM exposure already in the shortlist. |

Both retain positive alpha, but **neither offers a geography/style not better covered by a shortlisted fund**, and both lag their own peers — the Stage-2 disqualifier.
