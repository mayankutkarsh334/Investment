# Stage 2 — Performance / Fitness Filter

**Input:** 11 Stage-1 survivors (≥36-month track record).
**Result: 11 → 6.**

---

## Why Stage 2 runs on a 3-year basis

The study plan re-specified Stage 2 for a risk-reduction product: raw CAGR is the wrong primary ranker. With the age filter replaced by a ≥36mo floor, most survivors lack 5Y history — so Stage 2 runs on the **3-year horizon** (available for all 11) with a **risk-adjusted co-primary** (Sharpe). It is applied as a **fitness floor**: a fund is eliminated only if it is below **both** the 3Y-CAGR median **and** the Sharpe median — removing funds weak on *every* axis while keeping those strong on at least one.

## The eliminations, examined

- **ICICI Pru Multi-Asset — the headline.** The ₹84,000 Cr category flagship, longest record, fails **both** floors: 3Y 15.7% (below median) and Sharpe 0.29 (lowest but one). Its prominence is AUM and brand, not risk-adjusted quality. **Retained as a flagged instructive/reference case** — "why does the category's largest, oldest fund screen out?" is one of the most instructive questions in the study.
- **Edelweiss Multi Asset** — the most conservative survivor (22% eq / 52% debt, stdDev 2.6%) is essentially a debt fund: 3Y 7.7% (lowest), Sharpe exactly at the median. Out — but documented as the "too-conservative, basically-a-debt-fund" end of the spectrum.
- **HDFC Multi-Asset** — weakest on both among the veterans (3Y 12.4%, Sharpe 0.20); benchmarks NIFTY 50 TRI. Clean elimination + the flattering-benchmark instructive case.
- **Tata & Baroda** — middling on both axes, no axis of strength. Documented near-misses.

## Borderline / honesty notes

- **UTI passes on 3Y (16.5%) despite a weak Sharpe (0.36)** — the fitness floor lets it through on the return axis. Flagged as the **weakest-quality pass**; its Module 2 (risk) will be the decisive test of whether it belongs.
- **SBI passes on risk-adjusted quality** (Sharpe 0.93) despite a 3Y a hair below median — the mirror image of UTI.
