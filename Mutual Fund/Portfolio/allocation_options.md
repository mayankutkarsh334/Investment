## 8. Cross-Sleeve Risks, Triggers & Mechanics

**AMC concentration (watch when combining sleeves):**
- **BOI appears twice** — BOI Flexi Cap (Alok Singh) *and* BOI Small Cap (different manager, same AMC).
  Holding both in Full-split archetypes concentrates in one small PSU AMC. Alok Singh's exit is the
  named trigger that *ends the BOI FlexiCap thesis*.
- **Edelweiss can appear three times** (Flexi #4, Small #4, Mid #1) — all carry the AMC's noted
  regulatory-pattern flag. Fine as the single MidCap pick; be deliberate before stacking it across sleeves.

**Per-fund triggers (from the trees):**
- **Nippon MidCap** (if chosen over Edelweiss): monitor the **₹47K Cr capacity clock**.
- **International:** single-fund (Franklin US) by necessity — studies frozen. Watch for the SEBI
  overseas cap reopening (would re-enable cheap passive index funds — see `International/README.md`).
- **Excluded, do not add:** Quant, Invesco, HDFC FlexiCap, HSBC/ICICI/Sundaram MidCap.

**Mechanics:**
- **Exit loads / tax:** SmallCap & MidCap typically carry 1% exit load < 1yr; LTCG > 1yr. Don't
  churn the satellites — they need the full horizon.
- **Rebalancing:** review sleeve weights ~annually; top up laggards rather than selling winners to
  stay tax-efficient. Small/Mid will drift *up* in bull runs — trimming back to target is the
  discipline that harvests the risk premium.
- **SIP dates:** stagger across the month if you want finer cost-averaging (optional).
