# Deep Study Plan: 6-Module Framework

## Context
9 funds shortlisted after Stage 1 + Stage 2 screening. Each fund is studied across 6 modules before a final scored comparison.

---

## The 6 Modules

### Module 1 — Returns Consistency *(CSV data)*
- CAGR 3Y / 5Y / 10Y — durable across time horizons or lucky single period?
- 3Y Rolling Average — smoothed view across market cycles
- Absolute returns 1Y / 6M / 3M — recent momentum
- Alpha — skill or benchmark beta?
- Returns vs sub-category across all periods — consistent peer outperformance?

### Module 2 — Risk Profile *(CSV data)*
- Volatility vs category standard deviation
- Max Drawdown — worst historical fall; could you have stayed invested?
- Sharpe & Sortino ratios — return quality per unit of risk
- Tracking Error — how active is the manager's bet-taking?

### Module 3 — Portfolio DNA *(CSV data)*
- Largecap / Midcap / Smallcap split — risk style
- Top 10 holdings concentration — diversified or big bets?
- PE ratio vs category — value tilt vs growth tilt
- Cash holding — how defensive is the manager?
- % away from ATH — proximity to peak

### Module 4 — Cost & AUM Impact *(CSV data + calculation)*
- ₹20K/month SIP simulation over 10 years at actual ER
- AUM size and its structural impact on agility

### Module 5 — Fund Manager Research *(web research)*
- Current manager tenure on this specific fund
- Did the manager who built the track record change?
- Investment philosophy and style consistency

### Module 6 — AMC Quality *(web research)*
- SEBI regulatory actions or controversies
- Governance and investor communication quality
- AMC reputation in the industry

## Final Scoring Rubric

Score each fund 1–5 on each module, apply weights, sum for final rank.

| Module | Weight |
|--------|--------|
| Returns consistency (Module 1) | 25% |
| Risk-adjusted returns (Module 2) | 20% |
| Cost efficiency (Module 4) | 20% |
| Portfolio fit for SIP (Module 3) | 15% |
| Fund manager quality (Module 5) | 15% |
| AMC trustworthiness (Module 6) | 5% |

**Scale:** 1 = Poor, 2 = Below Average, 3 = Average, 4 = Good, 5 = Excellent
