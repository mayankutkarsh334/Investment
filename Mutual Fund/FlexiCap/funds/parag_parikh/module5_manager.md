## Investment Philosophy

- **Bottom-up, long-term value investing** — inspired by Benjamin Graham and Warren Buffett
- Core principle: identify high-quality companies at reasonable valuations with "margin of safety"
- Contrarian approach: willing to hold underperforming positions for years if conviction is high
- Extended holding periods — portfolio turnover ~20% (industry avg is 50–80%)
- "Local Fund with Global Focus" — 65% domestic (tax efficiency) + up to 35% international
