## Reputation

**Known for:**
- "India's Warren Buffett" philosophy — founded on value investing principles
- One of the most ethically run AMCs in India
- Prioritises investor alignment over asset gathering (historically ran very few schemes)
- Transparent communication — detailed factsheets, manager letters, regular blogs

**Notable characteristics:**
- For 12 years (2013–2025), ran only a handful of schemes — founder believed in keeping it simple
- Launched 7th scheme (Large Cap Fund) in January 2026 — first new fund in many years
- Some interpret this as minor departure from founder's philosophy of simplicity; others see it as natural growth

## International Exposure: RBI Restriction Status

| Detail | Status |
|--------|--------|
| International allocation | ~11.5% (Alphabet, Microsoft, Amazon, Meta) |
| SEBI limit on overseas investments | Up to 35% of AUM |
| Current RBI restriction | Fresh investments in overseas stocks are restricted |
| Impact | Cannot add to or replace international holdings |
| Trend | International % will gradually decline as fund AUM grows and no fresh overseas buying occurs |

This is a structural constraint unique to PPFAS — other Indian flexi-cap funds don't have significant international exposure and thus aren't affected by RBI overseas limits.
